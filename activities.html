Activities & Things to Do

Discover the many ways to enjoy your stay at Souškovo panství

Outdoor Activities

Hiking & Walking

The Ore Mountains (Krušné hory) offer countless hiking trails for all fitness levels. Explore the surrounding forests, meadows, and mountain paths right from the cottage doorstep.

  • Scenic forest walks
  • Mountain peak hikes
  • Nature photography
  • Bird watching

Cycling

Bring your bikes or rent them locally. The area features both challenging mountain bike trails and gentle countryside routes perfect for families.

Winter Sports

During winter months, enjoy cross-country skiing and downhill skiing at nearby resorts. The cottage is perfectly located for winter sports enthusiasts.

  • Cross-country skiing trails nearby
  • Downhill skiing resorts within short drive
  • Snowshoeing adventures
  • Sledding and winter fun

At the Cottage

Relaxation

  • Enjoy the peaceful garden and outdoor seating
  • Cozy evenings by the fire pit (winter)
  • Movie nights with the projector
  • Reading and board games

Cooking & Dining

The fully equipped kitchen allows you to prepare your own meals. Enjoy local ingredients from nearby markets or try traditional Czech recipes.

Nearby Attractions

Local Villages & Towns

Explore charming Czech and German border towns, each with their own unique character, local restaurants, and cultural sites.

Scenic Viewpoints

Drive or hike to various viewpoints offering stunning panoramas of the Ore Mountains and surrounding countryside.

Cultural Sites

Visit historical sites, museums, and local churches that tell the story of this border region's rich history.
