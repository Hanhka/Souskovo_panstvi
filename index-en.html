A cozy countryside cottage in Nová Ves v Horách, Czechia – perfect for peaceful escapes, family time, and exploring the Ore Mountains.

Contact / Book

About the cottage

Souškovo panství is a traditional cottage in the quiet village of Nová Ves v Horách, close to the German border in the Ore Mountains (Krušné hory). Surrounded by forests, meadows and hiking trails, it’s ideal for guests who want nature, fresh air and a relaxed atmosphere.

Amenities

  • Fully equipped kitchen
  • A cozy bedroom with a double bed
  • Comfortable living room with a projector
  • Fire pit for perfect winter atmosphere
  • Private garden with outdoor seating and grill for BBQ
  • Parking next to the cottage
  • Cross country skiing, downhill skiing very close
