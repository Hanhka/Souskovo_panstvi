The Village of Nová Ves v Horách

Nová Ves v Horách, located in the Ústí nad Labem Region, has a rich history dating back centuries. The village sits in the Ore Mountains (Krušné hory), a region known for its mining heritage and beautiful natural landscapes.

The cottage itself reflects the traditional architecture of the region, built to withstand the mountain climate while providing comfort to its inhabitants. Over the years, it has been lovingly maintained and updated to preserve its character while adding modern amenities.
